# Photos on the website: upload once, click the subject

From October 2026 every photo on the site is cut to shape automatically.
You upload one picture, click once on the most important point in it,
and the website makes every size and shape it needs (wide banners, cards,
round portraits) from that one click. There are no more hand-made
letterbox images.

## Adding or changing a photo

1. Upload the **largest original you have**. Do not resize or crop it
   first. JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP files are accepted. Phone photos are
   fine.
2. In the image form, **click the subject** on the preview (a face, the
   conductor, the centre of the group). A small marker shows where you
   clicked. This is the focal point: every crop is made so that this
   point stays in view and as near the centre as the shape allows.
3. Use the **Preview** link under the image to see how the photo will
   look in each shape (wide 16:9, card 3:2, square 1:1). If a head is
   cut off in one of them, move the click and look again.
4. Fill in the **Alternative text**: one sentence saying what is in the
   picture, for people who cannot see it. It is required.
5. Save. Nothing else to do; the site makes the sizes when a page first
   needs them.

## What happens behind the scenes

- The shapes are fixed: wide (16:9) for banners and heroes, card (3:2)
  for event and project cards, square (1:1) for people and logos.
- A small photo is **enlarged** to fit the shape rather than left small,
  so the layout stays even. It will look soft, so bigger originals are
  always better. Aim for at least 2000 pixels on the long side.
- Every size is delivered as **WebP**, a modern format about a third
  smaller than JPEG at the same quality, which every current browser
  reads. Your original file is kept untouched.
- Existing photos uploaded before October 2026 have their focal point in
  the centre until someone edits them and clicks the subject.

## If a photo looks wrong

Edit the media item (Content > Media, find the photo, Edit), move the
focal point and save. The pages update on their own within a minute or
two; if not, the team member who administers the site can clear the
cache.
